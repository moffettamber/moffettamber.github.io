--- layout: single permalink: / title: "Amber Moffett" author_profile: false --- I am a researcher at Monash University studying democratic erosion and authoritarianism. My work sits at the intersection of applied research, program strategy, and policy-relevant analysis in politically sensitive and conflict-affected environments, with a thematic focus on democracy, rights, and governance (DRG), post-conflict gender equality, and human security. Since 2015 I have led and coordinated cross-cultural research teams of 5–12 people across the United States, Myanmar, East Africa, and Colombia, conducting mixed-method and qualitative research — including interviews, document analysis, and comparative political analysis — for donors, policymakers, and practitioners in complex and emergency contexts. ## Current - Researcher, Monash University — democratic erosion and authoritarianism - Project Coordinator, Monash Global Peace and Security (GPS) - Senior Gender Specialist (consultant), World Bank Global Shield Financing Facility I also work as an [independent consultant](/consulting/) on gender integration in disaster risk finance and insurance.
