## Policy research reports

**Lead author**

- *Shan State Political Economic Analysis.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2022.
- *Women's Rights and Justice.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2021.
- *Burmanization.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2020. An examination of the perceptions, driving forces, and impacts of Burmanization in Myanmar, triangulating primary data with literature on national identity, ethnic relations, and the peace process.
- *Cultural Influences on Myanmar's National Identity.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2020. Explores how national identity manifests across eight cultural pillars, drawing on key informant interviews, focus groups, and social media analysis.
- *Migration, Environment and Climate Change Project Review.* Geneva, Switzerland: IOM Development Fund, 2016. A review of fifteen IOM-funded climate migration projects, assessing alignment with the MDGs/SDGs, gender mainstreaming, and sustainability.

**Contributing author**

- *Burmese Diaspora in the US.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2021.
- *Economic Growth in Myanmar: An Overview of Who Benefits from the Transition.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2020.
- *Freedom of Expression.* Yangon, Myanmar: Kimetrica, Mekong Economics, and USAID, 2019.
